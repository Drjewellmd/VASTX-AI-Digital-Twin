PROJECT:
VASTX (Virtual Astronaut Simulator for Therapeutics and Exploration): AN AI-Driven Digital Twin for Precision Drug DOsing and advancing Space Physiological-Based Pharmacokinetics (PBPK) in Space Medicine and improve state-of-the-art in predictive, preventitive medicine for applications in terrestrial and extreme environments on Earht. 

OVERVIEW:
VASTX (Virtual Astronaut Simulator for Therapeutics and Exploration) is an AI-driven digital twin platform designed to support safe, interpretable, and personalized medical decision-making in high-risk, resource-constrained environments.
These environments include:
- space missions (microgravity, delayed communication)
- submarines and underwater habitats
- military and humanitarian operations
-offshore oil rigs and industrial sites
- remote and underserved communities
In these settings, traditional healthcare infrastructure is limited, and decisions must be made under uncertainty, cognitive stress, and constrained resources.
VASTX addresses this gap by integrating physiologically based pharmacokinetic (PBPK) modeling, machine learning, and governance-constrained AI systems to simulate and predict human physiological responses and support safe decision-making.

CORE CONCEPTS:
VASTX combines three key components:
1. Digital Twin Modeling
- Individualized physiological simulation using PBPK models
- Predicts drug absorption, distribution, metabolism, and excretion

2. AI / Machine Learning Layer
- Learns patterns from physiological, behavioral, and environmental data
- Supports prediction of risk, response variability, and system states

3. Governance-Constrained Decision Framework
- Ensures outputs are bounded, interpretable, and safety-aligned
- Integrates with multi-agent AI systems (e.g., MAGSBHO)
- Enforces escalation to human oversight in high-risk conditions

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE (High-Level)
INPUTS:
- Physiological data (e.g., biomarkers, wearable sensor data)
- Behavioral signals (e.g., stress, cognitive load)
- Environmental conditions (e.g., microgravity, isolation, temperature)
- Mission and operational context

MODEL COMPONENtS: 
- PBPK models for drug and physiological simulation
- Machine learning (ML) models for risk prediction and classification
- Governance layer for decision control and escalation

OUTPUTS:
- Predicted physiological and pharmacokinetic responses
- Risk scores and uncertainty estimates
- Decision-support recommendations (bounded and interpretable)
- Alerts and escalation signals for human oversight

USE CASES:
Space Medicine: drug dosing and physiological monitoring in microgravity
Military & Defense: medical decision support in austere and combat environments
Humanitarian & Disaster Response: triage and treatment support in low-resource settings
Industrial Safety: health monitoring in offshore, mining, and high-risk operations
Remote Healthcare: personalized care in underserved and isolated communities

PROJECT STATUS
This repository represents an early-stage system architecture and research prototype for VASTX.
- Core concepts and system design are actively under development
- Simulation-based validation is ongoing
- Integration with analog astronaut mission environments is planned
- Real-world validation will occur through in-person analog astronaut missions

RELATIONAHSIP TO ITHER SYSTEMS: 
VASTX is part of a broader ecosystem of governance-constrained AI systems:
- 1. MAGSBHO (Multi-Agent Governance System for Behavioral Health and Operations)
→ multi-agent AI governance framework for safety-critical decision-making

- 2. ISPS-VETA (Integrated Space Psychiatry System – Virtual Embodiment Tele-Psychiatrist Avatar)
→ AI-enabled behavioral health and triage system

Together, these systems form a multi-layered AI architecture integrating physiological, behavioral, and operational intelligence.
